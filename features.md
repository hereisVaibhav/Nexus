# Nexus Wellness: Future Feature Roadmap

This document outlines high-impact, professional features designed to maximize user retention, engagement, and utility, selected based on user feedback.

## 🤖 Phase 1: Deep AI Integration (The "Wow" Factor)

### 1. 💬 AI Wellness Coach Chat
**Why:** Plans break. Users need real-time adjustments.
**Feature:** 
- A chat interface (like ChatGPT) specifically context-aware of the user's plan.
- **User:** "I accidentally ate a burger. Fix my plan."
- **AI:** "Okay, let's switch tonight's dinner to a light salad and add 10 mins of cardio. Here is the modified plan."

### 2. 🧠 Mood & Energy Correlation
**Why:** Holistic health connects mind and body.
**Feature:** 
- Daily check-in: "How are you feeling?" (1-10 scale for Mood, Energy, Stress).
- **Insight Engine:** After 14 days, tell the user: *"You report 20% higher energy on days you eat Oatmeal."* or *"Your stress is higher on days you skip meditation."*

## 🤝 Phase 2: Social & Gamification (The "Viral" Loop)

### 3. 🔥 Dynamic Streak System (Duolingo Style)
**Why:** Loss aversion is a powerful motivator.
**Feature:** 
- "Streak Freeze" items (earn them by working out 7 days in a row).
- Different streaks for different tracks (Workout Streak, Meditation Streak).

### 4. 🏆 "Squads" & Challenges
**Why:** Everything is easier with friends.
**Feature:** 
- Create a group with friend.
- Shared goal: "Walk 100k steps this week together."
- Leaderboard for "Consistency" (not just performance, to keep it fair for beginners).
